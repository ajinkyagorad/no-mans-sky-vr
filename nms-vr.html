    HOW TO START — you wake on an unexplored world with an empty warp drive.

    1. A glowing path of pips and a chevron point at the nearest salvage site — walk or fly along it.

    2. Touch the site to claim resources and warp charge. Mine rocks with the trigger for more.

    3. At 30% charge, open the nav hologram on your left hand and warp to a new planet. At 90% you can cross to another star.
  

  

    QUEST 3 / VR

    Left stick — walk  ·  push it fully to sprint (or hold left grip)  ·  Right grip — jetpack

    Right stick — snap turn

    Right trigger — mining beam  ·  Left trigger — scanner pulse

    Left Y — open the holo nav on your hand  ·  Left X — surface / system / galaxy

    Point & right trigger — select  ·  Right A — engage warp  ·  Right stick — spin & zoom the map


    DESKTOP

    Shift sprint  ·  click mine  ·  R scan
